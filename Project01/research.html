Work as a Teaching Assistant

IT240: Database Administration

During my third and fourth semesters at JMU, I worked a TA for IT240, first for a section taught by prof Hamdan, and then a section taught by Dr. Rizvi. My experience as a TA for this class reinforced my experience with databases, and gave me experience in teaching concepts to other students.

IT333: Advanced Networking

Currently, I work as a TA for a section of IT333 taught by Dr. Salib. My experience as a TA for this class has helped me reinforce my knowledge of networking, and has allowed me to share my knowledge and tips for one of the most difficult classes in the IT major

Research

Low Power Physical Layer Security

My fourth semester at JMU, I worked as an undergraduate researcher alongside Dr. Hasan and Matthew Engels, a fellow student. We researched artificial noise in low power IOT systems, and presented a demo at the 2026 CCI Conference in Richmond VA.

Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) Simulations

The summer between my fourth and fifth semesters at JMU, I worked alongside Dr. Kibilda and Daniel Campos de Silva, a masters student at the arlington campus of VT, I worked on an NSF funded research project developing new and improved ISAC simulators for drones using 6g.
