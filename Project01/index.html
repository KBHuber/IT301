Project 1: Static Webpage

For this project, we are asked to create a five page static website with real content. I decided to make a site with short discussions of some of my interests and things and I have been up to in the past couple of years.

The School page covers the classes I've taken in the past five semesters at JMU, and the classes I was able to transfer in.

The Extracurriculars page will discuss some of the career related things and work I do, such as cybersecurity competitions, research, and internships.

The Skiing page is about skiing, my work as a ski instructor, and the friends I've made through skiing.

The My Car page is about my 1997 Saab 9000 Aero, a rare and little-known Swedish sports car from the 90's.
