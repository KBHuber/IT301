Saab Facts

My car which I drive everywhere is a 1997 Saab 9000 Aero, a very rare sports car from the 90's. It was built by defense and aerospace company Saab, who at the time also made cars, to compete with the other large european luxury sports sedans of its time, such as the BMW 5 series, Audi A6, Jaguar XJ, and the Mercedes-Benz E-Class. It held the record of fastest front wheel drive production car from 1993-1999, dethroned only by Saab's next model, the 9-3. Early advertising talked about the car's ability to out-accelerate a Ferrari Testarossa and a Porsche 911 Carrera in the 50-70 mph range, which is useful for passing on the interstate.
